Biography

A career in chapters.

Selected milestones from a body of work that has moved deliberately from television to film — presented here for the benefit of prospective guests.

1980

Newcastle upon Tyne

Born Charles Matthew Hunnam on 10 April 1980. His parents divorced when he was a toddler, and he was raised largely by his mother, a business owner. At twelve he moved with her to Melmerby in Cumbria.

1998

Byker Grove & the First Break

Spotted by a production manager while shopping, he made his television debut in the BBC teen drama Byker Grove — the first step in a career that would soon move far beyond the North East.

1999 — 2000

Queer as Folk

His portrayal of Nathan Maloney in Russell T Davies’ ground-breaking series made him a household name in Britain and established him as an actor willing to take risks.

2002 — 2003

Nicholas Nickleby & Cold Mountain

His first leading film role came in Nicholas Nickleby, followed by a pivotal part in Anthony Minghella’s Cold Mountain — the first of what Hunnam calls his “trilogy of mad men.”

2005 — 2006

Green Street & Children of Men

Green Street Hooligans and Alfonso Cuarón’s Children of Men completed the trilogy and caught the attention of Kurt Sutter, who was casting a new series about an outlaw motorcycle club.

2008 — 2014

Sons of Anarchy — Jax Teller

As Jackson “Jax” Teller, Hunnam became the face of one of the most acclaimed dramas on American television. The role earned him two Critics’ Choice Television Award nominations and a permanent place in the cultural landscape.

2013

Pacific Rim

Guillermo del Toro cast him as Raleigh Becket in Pacific Rim — his first starring role in a major commercial film, and the beginning of a long collaboration with del Toro.

2017

King Arthur & The Lost City of Z

He led Guy Ritchie’s King Arthur: Legend of the Sword and starred as Percy Fawcett in James Gray’s The Lost City of Z — two very different visions of the adventurer, both anchored by his quiet intensity.

2019 — 2023

The Gentlemen, Triple Frontier & Rebel Moon

A run of varied roles: Guy Ritchie’s The Gentlemen, J.C. Chandor’s Triple Frontier, and Zack Snyder’s Rebel Moon franchise — proof that his range extends from crime to science fiction.

2025

Monster — Ed Gein

In the third season of Ryan Murphy’s Netflix anthology Monster, Hunnam took on the role of serial killer Ed Gein — a performance that critics have called his most transformative to date.
