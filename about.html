About

The man behind the roles.

Charlie Hunnam is an English actor whose career has moved from the working-class streets of Newcastle to the centre of American television and international film — driven by a quiet intensity that has made him one of the most compelling leading men of his generation.

Born Charles Matthew Hunnam in Newcastle upon Tyne, he moved to Cumbria at twelve after his mother remarried. He has described his teenage years there as “a crucible of violence and isolation,” finding solace in the Lake District and in the films of John Boorman. A chance encounter at seventeen led to his first television role, and by 1999 he had become a household name in Britain with Queer as Folk.

The defining role came in 2008, when Kurt Sutter cast him as Jackson “Jax” Teller in Sons of Anarchy. Over seven seasons, Hunnam turned a motorcycle-club prince into a Shakespearean figure of loyalty and violence, earning two Critics’ Choice nominations and a permanent place in television history. Since then he has worked with Guillermo del Toro, Guy Ritchie, James Gray and Zack Snyder — always drawn to characters who carry a certain weight.
